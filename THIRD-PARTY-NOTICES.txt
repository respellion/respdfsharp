Embedded fonts
--------------

The ResPdfSharp assembly embeds the following fonts for the built-in fallback
font resolver (PdfSharp.Fonts.BundledFontResolver). Each is shipped in four
styles (regular, bold, italic, bold italic) as TrueType, and the corresponding
OFL license text is embedded as an assembly resource, retrievable at runtime
via BundledFontResolver.GetFontLicense(family).

  Arimo (Regular, Bold, Italic, BoldItalic)
    Copyright 2026 The Arimo Project Authors
    https://github.com/googlefonts/Arimo
    Metric-compatible substitute for Arial / Helvetica.

  Tinos (Regular, Bold, Italic, BoldItalic)
    Copyright 2026 The Tinos Project Authors
    https://github.com/googlefonts/tinos
    Metric-compatible substitute for Times New Roman.

  Cousine (Regular, Bold, Italic, BoldItalic)
    Copyright 2026 The Cousine Project Authors
    https://github.com/googlefonts/cousine
    Metric-compatible substitute for Courier New.

All three fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1
(reproduced in full below and embedded in the assembly).

  - The fonts are not sold by themselves; they are distributed only as part
